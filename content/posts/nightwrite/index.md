<p>Nightwrite is a made-up word about making things up.</p><p>It’s a tiny dose of creativity<br>each day<br>before bed.</p><p>The idea is to take a brief moment<br>as the day draws to a close<br>to let your mind wander<br>and make room for the smallest expression<br>of something that doesn’t yet exist.</p><p>Maybe it’s a rhythmic line of poetry<br>an incomplete lyric<br>opening scene<br>or snippet of dialogue.</p><p>Write a few sentences. Maybe doodle or sketch.</p><p>No need to polish. No need to title the future novel.</p><p>Each night, that moment belongs to you.</p><p>And what you do next does, too.</p><p>You might take these small creations and<br>post them for the world to see<br>or throw them out<br>or tuck them in a safe place.</p><p>Maybe you’ll fill the front of your fridge or text them to that friend who so enjoys this part of you.</p><p>It's tempting to see creativity as a means to an end, a way to check a box or accomplish a goal.</p><p>“What are you working on?”</p><p>“What's it for?”</p><p>But, sometimes the purpose isn't a product. It's to find a way to say what you couldn’t say any other way.</p><p>Sometimes, it’s just for you.</p><blockquote>It came without ribbons.<br>It came without tags.<br>It came without packages, boxes or bags.</blockquote><p>Creativity remixes people and ideas and moments with our truest selves. The result is something unique and valuable, regardless of whether others see it as mundane or magical.</p><p>For me, nightwrite is a small reminder to embrace the made up and frivolous, unpolished and poetic. Maybe it will be for you, too.</p>
