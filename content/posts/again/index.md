<p>I love to think about words and lately one has been playing over and over in my mind.</p><p><strong>Again.</strong></p><p>The more I think about it, the more so much of life seems to flow through that short, simple word. Again is in many ways how we define what we love.</p><p>I listen to an album for the first time and as I do, I wonder, "Do I want to listen to this again?"</p><p>After I try a new restaurant, the first question is, "Would I go there again?"</p><p>A friend goes to a concert. "How was it? Would you see them again?"</p><p>That wine was delicious. Next time I'm here, I'll order it again.</p><p>It was an incredible book. I definitely want to read it again.</p><p>It was such a fun vacation. I already want to go back again.</p><p>The movie was amazing. I can't wait to see it again.</p><p>That game is great! Let's play again.</p><p>Again doesn't just define the things and experiences we love, though. It's part of the decisions that define who we are.</p><p>Do you want to go out with her again?</p><p>Would you marry him again?</p><p>If you could start over,</p><p>would you do it all</p><p>again?</p>
