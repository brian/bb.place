<p>Today is Thanksgiving in the U.S. It's a unique holiday set aside for appreciating the abundance all around us. Many travel long distances to be with friends and family (in fact, my sister and her family have joined us from Michigan). There is often a full house and a table overflowing with food, new faces and ones you've always known.</p><p>As I reflect on this year, I'm enormously grateful for this community of truly uncommon people. Sometimes I'm lucky enough to sit across from one of you for an hour or two. Last week,  <a href="http://wesleyverhoeve.com/">Wesley</a> came to town and I was reminded again of the overflowing talent and creativity that exists within Uncommon, but even more, of a certain way of going about things; a kindness and openness, a heart for exploration and desire to bring others into the story. Each of these encounters, and each reply, helps me see a world full of possibility. Thank you ever so much.</p><p>I hope wherever this dispatch finds you, and whether you celebrate Thanksgiving or not, that this week includes quiet moments of gratitude. And let's remember that many in our midst are hurting right now; heartache, a scary diagnosis, struggling to make rent, and many things in-between. May there be room at our tables for everyone.</p><h3 id="prompted">Prompted</h3><p><a href="https://bb.place/uncommon/story/the-air-of-belonging">Last week's dispatch</a> asked, <em>Is there a moment or person you're especially grateful for this year?</em></p><p><a href="https://twitter.com/ResonanceGlobal">Radhika</a> wrote:</p><blockquote>I was going to write about one thing, but I've decided to write about something else...
