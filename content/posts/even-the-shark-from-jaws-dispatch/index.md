<p><em>An Evening with Kevin Smith</em> includes a story about Ben Affleck. The person who was casting the movie <em>Daredevil</em> asked Kevin Smith who should play the lead.</p><blockquote>I said, "Affleck" because that's my answer for everything. I'm a big fan, so I think he can play anything. If people are like, "Jaws?" I'm like, "Affleck. Affleck plays the .... shark."</blockquote><blockquote>I told Affleck, "There's a dude checking if you wanna play Daredevil." He's like, "I love Daredevil." I said, "That's what I told him."</blockquote><blockquote>He said, "What'd you say?" I said, "You should play it." He said, "Why?" I was like, "I think you should play everything."</blockquote><blockquote>"Like, even the shark from Jaws?"</blockquote><blockquote>"Yes, the shark from Jaws."</blockquote><p>I love this so much and think about it nearly every day. Wrapped in the hilarious dialogue and delivery is one of the most powerful forces on the planet: one person's complete, unwavering confidence in another.</p><p>Every day I'm struck by the talent of friends and people in this community. We're surrounded by people undeniably capable of big, adventurous things: beginning a new life in a new country, writing a book, learning programming, mentoring students, starting a business, caring for a sick loved one, teaching elementary school, speaking in front of 30 friends or 300 strangers, traveling the world, leading a team, being a good neighbor under difficult circumstances, and much more.</p><p>Sometime we forget and need to be reminded, though. We don't always see what others see. I'm thankful for friends who have stepped in at just the right moment and provided the perspective I've lost. I try to do the same. There are few better gifts to give or receive.</p><p><em>You should know that I'm a big fan. I think you should play everything.</em></p><p><em>Even the shark from Jaws.</em></p><h3 id="your-turn">Your Turn</h3><p>Let's try a small Uncommon community project. Do you know someone who is unsure of a new adventure, someone who doesn't see themselves the way you see them? Seek them out and tell them of your complete, unwavering confidence. Let know know that you believe they are capable of anything. Your words may be exactly what they need to hear at just the right moment.</p><p>Then, feel free to reply with your story or just enjoy the moment.
