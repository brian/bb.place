<p>I hope March has started well for you, wherever you're reading this. Spring arrived in Austin and brought with it the pleasures of porches and patios, open windows and long walks.</p><p>Thanks for taking a few minutes out of your day to catch up on news from the Uncommon neighborhood.</p><p>As I mentioned in the latest dispatch, prompts are now included in stacks! Now, when you stop by, there's a question that might inspire you to reply (and you don't even need to send an email). The simple spark and opportunity to contribute makes for a much different experience. There are nearly 150 prompts now, so there's a plethora of thought-provoking questions awaiting you.</p><p>What's next? We're working on a new homepage and member dispatch, plus a project that is very exciting, but probably too new to talk about :) We're also starting to explore uncommon ways for members to interact and share more than favorites and prompt replies.</p><p>A huge thank you to everyone who is contributing their time and talent to bring these additions to life. Every conversation has been an absolute pleasure and ends with fresh insights and connections with uncommon neighbors near and far.</p><p>If you haven't joined one yet, Table for Six is a telephone conversation with six members that lasts for about an hour. It begins with a topic, but like all good conversations, can go in surprising directions. Everyone is welcomed and made to feel at home.</p><p>Our theme for this edition is More or Less: We often chase more and then find ourselves wanting less. In that ebb and flow between scarcity and abundance, how do you balance pursuing and letting go of things, whether objects, relationships, information, goals, or anything else?</p><p>What’s particularly fun about this round is Uncommon members, Sam and Ryan, will be hosting two of the calls (and others are eagerly awaiting the chance to host soon).
