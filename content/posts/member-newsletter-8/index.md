<p>Hello, friends!</p><p>A month ago, Uncommon's front porch debuted. Thanks to each of you for stopping by and making yourself at home. It's been truly wonderful to learn a bit more about the people who make up this community and begin to see new favorite things. We especially appreciate the kind words and helpful critiques. We've addressed a few problem spots and have more in the works before the public debut.</p><p>The best part of the past month has been new people arriving from your invitations (followed closely by discovering your many creative uses of the 3-word bio). We hope you've enjoyed that uniquely tangible experience. And a huge welcome to the new faces in our midst. We're so glad you're here.</p><p>Recently, we finished adding the prompt replies of every member to the site. Over two years, you've shared 576 replies. This is a treasured collection of insights and stories; profound, hilarious, and everything in-between. You'll find them whenever you refresh the homepage. There will be a more elegant way to experience those in the future :)</p><p>Would you like to see a few fine examples of Uncommon so far? Meet Melissa, Phil and etherbrian, then browse a favorite thing, like coffee or reading.</p><p>Next up is opening Uncommon to everyone and preparing the second edition of Tangibly Uncommon. In the meantime, please continue to pass along any thoughts or questions, no matter how small. We love hearing from you.</p><p>Wishing you a month filled with great conversations and delightful surprises.</p><p>Cheers,</p><p>Brian</p><p>Tip: Click the camera icon whenever you come across it. The first prompt replies were actual postcards we received in the mail. The cards themselves tell their own story.</p>
