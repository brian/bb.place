<p>In our always-on, real-time, infinite scroll world, why would a website periodically close?</p><p>On Friday evening each week, the Uncommon site goes quiet for 24 hours. During that time, someone who stops by, whether a member or not, sees a lovely page explaining that the site is currently closed. We call it sunset.</p><p>Sunset reminds us to find inspiration beyond our screens; to stop for a moment, close the laptop, and put down the phone. To make room for meandering conversations with friends, find new paths through familiar neighborhoods, and be fully present in the moment.</p><p>Over the past few years, I’ve read article after article about how to have a healthy relationship with technology, how to find balance and resist the addictive pull.</p><p>Many sites and services, though, are specifically designed to overcome our resistance. Success is when someone wonders where the last few hours went. And our resistance is lowest when we’re tired from a long week of work and constant decision-making, grocery shopping and classes, daycare logistics and figuring out how to make rent. Like on Friday nights, for example.</p><p>The myriad of think pieces and essays have a common thread: the onus is on us. If only we have sufficient willpower, change this setting, try a little harder, we can thwart the proven methods of the most effective, well-funded companies around.</p><p>I don’t look at it that way. We have responsibility, of course, but let’s not pretend it’s a fair fight. At some point, the people who make decisions about how sites and apps work have to take a stand on behalf of the people who use them.</p><p>That’s all the sunset page is, just a whisper amidst the noise: <em>We’re on your side.</em></p><h3 id="prompted">Prompted</h3><p><a href="https://bb.place/uncommon/story/surpassed-expectations">The latest dispatch</a> asked, <em>Do you have a favorite team, past or present?</em></p><p><a href="https://twitter.com/schnizzy">Elizabeth</a> wrote:</p><blockquote>My favorite team at the moment, without a doubt, is West Ham United Football Club.
