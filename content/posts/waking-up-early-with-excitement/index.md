<p>Why a front porch?</p><p>I love playing with metaphors, but what does it mean to call Uncommon a <em>front porch</em> for the Internet?</p><p>When I think of a porch, I imagine friends and neighbors talking and laughing together. People sit on the steps, lean against the wall, and relax in one of the misfit chairs. A lucky pair temporarily commandeers the porch swing.</p><p>There’s a cool breeze as the bright afternoon turns to dusk. A few people drift off to other adventures and others take their place. Each person brings something to share, from a special bottle or dish to an unforgettable story.</p><p>Most arrive with someone, but minutes later, they're drawn into conversations with new friends. They’re curiosity is piqued by books, movies, albums, exhibits, and restaurants they are eager to explore. As darkness settles, the talk grows quieter and more reflective. Unusual candor and intimate conversation spark shifts in perspective. The thread that brought everyone together is strengthened.</p><p>That explains what we mean by <em>porch</em>, but why a <em>front porch</em> and not a <em>back porch?</em></p><p>Normally when you find yourself on someone’s back porch, you were invited. In many cases, you’re already friends.</p><p>A front porch, though, welcomes the passersby; the stranger who just moved in across the street and the neighbor who you’ve only shared polite waves with. The gathering is in the open and the sound of music and smells of delicious food waft down the sidewalk. It's as easy to stop by for a few as it is to stay for hours.</p><p>Everyone is welcome.</p><h3 id="prompted">Prompted</h3><p><a href="/uncommon/story/wondering-what-magic-will-happen">The last dispatch</a> asked, <em>What were your favorite toys growing up?</em></p><p><a href="https://twitter.com/theshaggyviking">Jason</a> wrote:</p><blockquote>I have to say that growing up I loved my <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Trak">Big Trak</a>! We brought together groups of 6 members to talk about things big and small. People joined from Southampton, San Francisco, Berlin, NYC, Malmö, Washington D.C. and places in-between. Our topic of choice was Back and Forth, from travel adventures to routine commutes.</p><p>And you know, it was amazing. People described the experience as “unusual”, “inspiring”, “fulfilling”, “peculiarly refreshing”, and “energizing.” The front porch came to life :)</p><p>One conversation felt like a high-energy brunch. There were hilarious, elaborate stories of epic road trips and vacation catastrophes. It was incredibly fun.</p><p>Another had the vibe of a lingering dinner. I found myself jotting down insightful things people were saying. It felt like it could gone on and on and a few people mentioned they wished it had.</p><p>The phone turned out to be an essential element. We had considered video, but realized that we have plenty of time with our screens already.
