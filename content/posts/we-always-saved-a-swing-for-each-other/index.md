<p>Recent cold spells in Austin have left me addicted to fires in the fireplace. I've enjoyed them in the past, but I've never so enjoyed building them. The warmth and sounds are comforting, the flames mesmerizing. Starting the fire is a fun challenge, but keeping it going is the part I enjoy the most. It requires continual partial attention, a skill technology has given us in abundance.</p><p>I love the conversations that fires make possible. Long stretches of silence are awkward in normal conversation, so we have developed techniques to prevent them. Fires, though, are just distracting enough to make that silence tolerable, even pleasant. It's usually the conversations that happen after those stretches of silence that are the most meaningful.
