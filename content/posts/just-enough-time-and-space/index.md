<p>My father was a social worker and when I was born, we actually lived at a camp in Michigan which he ran. Every week during the summer, a new group of kids from Detroit would arrive at Franklin Settlement Camp. Located on the shore of a small lake, the camp was pretty much exactly what you'd imagine: a main building and cafeteria, a few large cabins, and a lot of room. My dad played guitar and each evening ended with songs around the campfire.</p><p>Even after we moved to a house a few blocks away and a slightly more normal life, the spirit of camp always felt close. Strangers drifting in and later returning as familiar faces, talking late into the night around the table or fireplace, music performances by the young and old, badminton in the backyard, and laughing and conspiring on the front porch of our 100-year old home, swaying on the swing until the quiet of darkness made our voices seem too loud.</p><p>When I revisit those memories, I see a lot of Uncommon — leisurely conversations, fleeting closeness, and peaceful surroundings. This is the fiftieth dispatch. Nearly one year ago, I <a href="/uncommon/story/a-community-of-possibilities">wrote a post asking</a> if there were others who thought it was time to rethink community online. I was terribly nervous.</p><p>Of course, it turned out that there are many of us. What a year it has been, a constant stream of inspiration, learning, and wonderful moments. The highlight has been the many stories of people within the community finding each other both online and off as introductions are made and new friendships formed. Here are some others...</p><ul><li>582 people reading, shaping, and contributing to Uncommon</li><li>100 founding members</li><li>100 welcome postcards mailed</li><li>174 postcards received in reply</li><li><a href="/uncommon/story/about">10 friends</a> giving their time and talent</li><li>539 Git commits and 167 Basecamp discussions</li><li>Our first donation to <a href="http://ag47collective.com/">Ag47 Collective</a></li><li><a href="https://twitter.com/keeg/status/281533134854565888/photo/1">The first Tangibly Uncommon</a> featuring an original print by <a href="https://twitter.com/etherbrian">@etherbrian</a></li><li>A site that welcomes curious newcomers in a uniquely beautiful way</li></ul><p>Meeting so many of you has been incredible. You are uncommon in every sense.</p><p>The year ahead is going to be a special one as we take these ideas and sketches and make them real.
