<p>Reading a music magazine years ago, I came across a stray sentence that set me on an obsessive quest. An article casually mentioned that David Lee Roth had recorded a Spanish-language version of his album, <em>Eat 'Em and Smile</em>.</p><p>The name was <em>Sonrisa Salvaje</em>.</p><p>I'm not entirely sure what captivated me about this obscure piece of information, but I knew immediately that I had to find this album. Something about the idea of Van Halen's lead singer recording an entire album in Spanish fascinated me. How could it be anything other than brilliant?</p><p>My search lasted multiple years. The album was sold for a limited amount of time on LP and cassette and was now, unsurprisingly, out-of-print. I scoured used record stores near and far (it was worth it just for the reactions of the staff when I explained what I was looking for), searched the far corners of the Internet (there were fewer corners then), and perused eBay regularly. I never came close.</p><p>One day, I mentioned my unrealized dream to a friend who hung out in Internet neighborhoods I wasn't allowed to frequent. A few weeks passed and then one day, he handed me a flash drive with a huge smile on his face.</p><p>To this day, I still can't believe that such a thing exists, that I have a copy, and that it's just so deliriously good; over-the-top guitar matched by over-the-top David Lee Roth, singing songs like <em>¡Loco Del Calor!</em> with unabashed gusto. The album has since been released on CD and MP3, which means my epic quest can be replicated in the time it takes to say <em>Así Es La Vida</em>.</p><p>There's nothing grand about <em>Sonrisa Salvaje</em>, but I have never heard anything like it. I find so much pleasure in its breathtaking absurdity.</p><p>The world needs more joy. Sometimes the result of high standards and chasing other people's definitions of quality is missing out on a goofy summer movie, kitschy restaurant, or life-altering story written for a different age group.</p><p>Wherever you find joy, however out of favor the source, drink up every last ounce. Enjoy it for what it is.
