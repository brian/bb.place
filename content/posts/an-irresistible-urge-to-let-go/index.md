<p>We have something new this week; the first Uncommon Conversation! Many of you have mentioned that you'd like to learn more about the wonderful people who make Uncommon what it is. We begin with a terrific conversation between Lisa and <a href="http://chinalily67.blogspot.com/">Mona</a>, a digital project manager and filmmaker in Vancouver. Mona is a founding member of Uncommon and a constant source of insight.</p><p><em>In an early dispatch, you described yourself as “a lifelong employee with a filmmaking hobby.” How did you discover filmmaking, and what do you love about it?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> The Vancouver Film School had a reduced introductory offer for their new Sound Design program for film. After graduating, I helped some friends with their sound editing and wound up wondering what directing would be like. Fast forward to a feature length documentary (<a href="http://www.letmebefictional.com/">Let Me Be Fictional</a>) about an indie rock band and a comedy (<a href="http://www.louderthaneverything.com/">Everything Louder Than Everything Else</a>) that premiered at the Whistler Film Festival. I am hooked on creating a mood, an experience that brings people into the journey of life.</p><p><em>What are some of the challenges you’ve encountered in filmmaking and story-writing?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> Besides the usual challenges of being a female, my biggest challenge is confidence in my ability to create something. This barrier has prevented me from starting projects, but I'm working on it.</p><p><em>If places were characters, which would play the leading roles in your story?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> Alas, it would be Vancouver. I've lived here for all but the first 3 years of my life and it's still interesting and exciting to me. From its diverse ethnic communities and hippie roots to its outdoorsiness and tech industry, Vancouver has multiple personalities to suit almost everyone.</p><p><em>You shared your love of organizational tools, especially <a href="https://trello.com/">Trello</a>, as one of your favorite things few others would understand. Do you have any advice for the hopelessly disorganized among us?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> Break your project into really small tasks that are much more manageable. Once you stop staring at the big, seemingly unattainable goal and instead focus on the tiny task, it can feel more achievable.</p><p><em>What are you most curious about lately?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> I'm returning to my roots as a sound editor and interested in creating downloadable auditory-only narratives that are specific to a particular street.</p><p><em>What's your favorite thing about the internet?</em></p><p><strong>Mona:</strong> It provides the opportunity to strengthen real life connections and to explore online connections based on shared interests.</p><h3 id="prompted">Prompted</h3><p><a href="https://bb.place/uncommon/story/drunk-in-love">Last week's dispatch</a> asked, <em>What was the last idea you fell in love with?</em></p><p><a href="https://twitter.com/duregger">Sam</a> wrote:</p><blockquote>I manufacture ideas… it's a blessing and a curse.
